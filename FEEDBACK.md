# Feedback

`pqc-inventory` is a **defensive static** scanner (source + dependency manifests → priority report + CycloneDX 1.6 CBOM). It does **not** certify compliance or migrate crypto.

## How to give feedback

1. Open a [Product feedback](https://github.com/18z/pqc-inventory/issues/new?template=feedback.yml) issue
2. Or start a Discussion (if enabled) under Ideas / Q&A
3. Or star the repo and comment on what you tried

## Useful reports

- Noise you want suppressed (hashes, comments, tests)
- Crypto you expected that was missed
- CBOM fields you need for procurement / EO 14412 readiness tracking
- Whether lifetime/exposure overrides match how you work

Please do not paste secrets, private keys, or proprietary source.
